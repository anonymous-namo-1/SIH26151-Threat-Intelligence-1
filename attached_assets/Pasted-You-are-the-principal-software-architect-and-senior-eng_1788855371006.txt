You are the principal software architect and senior engineering team for an SIH project based on SIH26151.

Build a COMPLETE, production-style full-stack intelligence investigation platform for correlating threat-actor personas, infrastructure, cryptocurrency indicators, documents, identities, activity patterns and other lawful intelligence.

This is not just a UI prototype.

Build the frontend, backend, APIs, databases, authentication, investigation engine, entity extraction pipeline, relationship graph, scoring system, AI analysis services, reports, audit logging, testing, Docker setup, seed data and documentation.

IMPORTANT SAFETY / SCOPE:
This platform is for lawful, authorized cybersecurity investigation and research.

Do NOT implement:
- exploitation of Tor or hidden services
- malware
- credential theft
- unauthorized system access
- deanonymization exploits
- IP leakage attacks
- phishing
- account takeover
- bypassing authentication
- destructive actions
- automated accusations that a real person is a criminal

The system may ingest authorized/public intelligence and analyze relationships between supplied data.

Every attribution must be represented as a hypothesis with evidence, confidence and uncertainty.

==================================================
1. PRODUCT
==================================================

Create a professional web application called:

"ARGUS"
Threat Intelligence Correlation & Investigation Platform

Purpose:

An investigator creates a CASE.

Inside the case they can enter or import things such as:

- usernames
- aliases
- email addresses from authorized datasets
- domains
- IP addresses from authorized/public intelligence
- onion addresses
- URLs
- cryptocurrency wallet addresses
- PGP fingerprints
- public keys
- forum posts
- marketplace listings
- chat excerpts
- documents
- timestamps
- social/profile links
- infrastructure indicators
- uploaded CSV/JSON
- manually entered evidence

ARGUS converts this information into entities and relationships.

Example:

PERSONA
    |
    +--- USERNAME
    |
    +--- PGP KEY
    |
    +--- WALLET
    |
    +--- POST
    |
    +--- DOMAIN
    |
    +--- INFRASTRUCTURE
    |
    +--- SECOND PERSONA

Investigators should be able to explore the resulting graph visually.
